Onboarding Checklist: WhatsApp Permissions

To connect this platform to WhatsApp, both permissions are required: whatsapp_business_messaging and whatsapp_business_management. This screen is a step-by-step checklist for onboarding operators.

Meta requirements used for this checklist:
  • As a direct developer use-case, app-level testing can work with default access for app roles (admin/developer).
  • As a solution provider onboarding other businesses, each permission must be requested as Advanced Access in App Review.
  • App Review requires written explanation + screen recording per requested permission.

1) whatsapp_business_messaging Messaging

This permission is needed for your app’s actual messaging functions: sending, receiving and tracking messages from conversations.

  • Send outbound message from Inbox Maps to outbound message flow in the plugin.
  • Receive incoming WhatsApp message in the unified inbox Requires webhook subscription and message processing. Live feature
  • Show delivery/read status updates Used for operational visibility in logs and history.
  • Attach a message-send demo for review Include a recording that clearly shows sending from your app.
Completed: 0/4

2) whatsapp_business_management Management

Needed to control WhatsApp business assets in your platform, which your onboarding already does: WABA, phone number, templates and webhook settings. Coexistence with the official WhatsApp Business app is also handled through this management permission.

  • Connect or onboard a WABA and business phone number Core onboarding completion step.
  • Create / edit and sync templates Used for approved template-based communication workflows. Templates
  • Read/update WABA/phone metadata Keeps account state in sync between Meta and your SaaS.
  • Set up and validate webhook subscription Required for inbound messages and status event updates.
  • Attach a management-use demo for review Include template creation or management action in a dedicated recording.
  • Enable WhatsApp coexistence mode This is part of the whatsapp_business_management scope and keeps the official WhatsApp app usable while routing conversations through your platform.
Completed: 0/6

Full onboarding prerequisites

  • Active Meta developer account with security requirements enabled.
  • Public legal pages in place and policy details aligned to your app use case.
  • Use case text in App Review clearly references the onboarding, templates and inbox flows.
  • One screen recording per permission in the App Review submission.
